    doom1.wad

    Arduino/ESP32Toy_DoomGeneric_Phase3/data/doom1.wad

When the LittleFS filesystem image is uploaded to the ESP32-S3 board,
this file becomes visible to the sketch as:

    /doom1.wad

    /littlefs/doom1.wad
